Knowledge Center

Insights, guides, and resources for automotive importers

What is the minimum order quantity?

Most brands require a minimum of 1 unit for sample orders. Bulk orders of 10+ units receive preferential pricing and priority shipping.

How long does shipping take?

Transit times vary by destination: Middle East 15-25 days, Africa 25-40 days, South America 30-45 days, Asia 7-20 days.

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes, we work with local customs brokers in each destination country to ensure smooth clearance and delivery.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept T/T (bank transfer), L/C (letter of credit), and Western Union. Terms are negotiable for repeat customers.

Can I visit the factories?

Absolutely! We organize factory visits for serious buyers. Contact us to schedule a trip to Chinese automotive manufacturing facilities.

What about after-sales support?

We provide technical support, spare parts supply chains, and warranty management through our network of local partners.
